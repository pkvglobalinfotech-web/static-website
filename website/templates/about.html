About PKV Global Infotech

PKV Global Infotech is a newly established digital product development and IT services company based in Tamil Nadu, India.

Our product engineering division focuses on developing specialised domain software — including Hospital Information Management systems, Human Resource platforms, and multi-module ERP software designed to connect core business workflows.

In parallel, our IT services team works directly with businesses to design and deploy modern websites, maintain existing web assets, build secure API integrations between business tools, and provide ongoing application maintenance.
